Engineering Focus & Philosophy

I am an Aerospace Engineering student at Penn State (Schreyer Honors College) working at the intersection of experimental aerodynamics, data acquisition, and computational modeling. My research at the GIMAS Lab focuses on passive boundary layer flow control and wind tunnel test section integration—deploying multi-sensor DAQ architectures and translating raw empirical test data into validated predictive models.I enjoy tackling problems where closed-form analytical solutions fall short and predictive computational models are required.

Growing up with a multilingual, multicultural background has shaped how I approach aerospace engineering: as a collaborative, borderless discipline driven by long-term impact. Looking forward, I aim to apply first-principles thinking and simulation-driven design to mission-critical aerospace architectures, helping design and validate the systems that will sustain exploration beyond Earth.

My long-term goal is to contribute to space technology and exploration through roles centered on modeling, simulation, and systems analysis supporting the design, validation, and optimization of aerospace systems. I am motivated by the challenge of enabling humanity's sustained presence beyond Earth through rigorous engineering, thoughtful design, and data driven decision making.
