Context & Motivation

Authored in March 2024 as an independent academic milestone evaluating the physical legitimacy of temporal traversal under General and Special Relativity[cite: 1]. Rather than treating backward and forward displacement as speculative fiction, the study evaluated empirical relativistic observations—including high-velocity Low Earth Orbit dilation (ISS ~0.0082s drift) and Hafele-Keating atomic clock experiments—before analyzing theoretical geometries permitted by Einstein’s field equations[cite: 1].

Analytical Frameworks Explored

  • Traversable Wormhole Dynamics: Analyzed Flamm’s wormholes and Morris-Thorne metrics, evaluating the structural requirement of negative energy densities (exotic matter) to counter extreme throat tidal forces and prevent singularity pinch-off[cite: 1].
  • Cosmic Strings & Closed Geodesics: Investigated relativistic string dynamics where rapid relative motion past parallel strings creates localized metric distortions that loop spacetime trajectories[cite: 1].
  • Gravitational Dilation Models: Formulated comparative variable-gravity systems (Y > g > X) to demonstrate how non-inertial observer frames shift temporal elapsed rates[cite: 1].
  • Closed Timelike Curves (CTCs): Explored Minkowski spacetimes with compactified temporal topologies, evaluating future-directed light cone trajectories looping back without locally violating the speed of light[cite: 1].
  • Paradox Resolution: Contrasted Novikov self-consistency constraints against Everett-style branching multiverse solutions for classical grandfather contradictions[cite: 1].
